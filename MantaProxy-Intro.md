## Three Proxy Types: Which One Fits Your Workflow?

**Static ISP proxies** suit users who need a fixed exit IP with ISP residential network characteristics. They're an option to consider for managing cross-border storefronts, handling day-to-day social media operations, and running tasks that require a consistent address over time.

**Static native residential proxies** place greater emphasis on the IP's geographic origin and its association with a local internet service provider. For localized operations, live streaming, or checking how content appears in a particular region, you can evaluate these resources against your location requirements.

**Datacenter proxies** suit general web access, network monitoring, and automated testing. If your target service accepts datacenter traffic and your priorities are efficiency and cost, this category is worth considering.
